# CHANGELOG MODULE ANX HR FOR [DOLIBARR ERP CRM](https://www.dolibarr.org)

## 1.0.0

Initial version:

- Module descriptor with permissions, HR top menu, HR tab on user card, dictionaries (deadline types, handover categories) and scheduled jobs.
- HR cockpit with KPIs (deadlines, checklists, time corrections, monthly periods, missing clock-out).
- HR tab on user card (contract, deadlines, emergency contacts, equipment and access, checklists, time of current month).
- Clock state badge in the top right menu.
- Deadline reminders (agenda event and notification `ANXHR_DEADLINE_REMIND`).
- Retention cleanup of time records after end of employment.
